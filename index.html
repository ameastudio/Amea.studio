FAQ

How long does an Améa piece take?

Standard made-to-order pieces can take up to two weeks. Your exact timing is shown during ordering.

Can I customize my piece?

Selected products can be customized. Eligible options and any additional cost are shown on the product page.

Pickup or delivery?

Both are supported. Available options and the fulfillment cost are shown before payment.

What is the returns policy?

Améa does not offer returns, exchanges or refunds. Eligible alterations may be available, and workmanship issues can be reviewed by Améa.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders may be cancelled from your account within 48 hours, subject to production/customization status.
